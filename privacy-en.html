Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how the YuNote (CarWash CRM) mobile app handles data. Developer: YuNote. For privacy questions, contact yunote.app@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account and business: name, phone number, a password hash used to verify your password, car wash name and address, employee count, and working hours.
  • Operations: employee names and commission rates, vehicle registration plates and types, services, washes, notes, prices, debts, payment records, and employee settlement records.
  • Notifications and device: if you enable push notifications, an installation identifier, Expo push token, device platform, app version, and notification preferences.

How we use data

We use data to provide account access and security, manage car wash records, calculate statistics, maintain debt and payment history, and deliver the notifications you choose. The camera feature reads registration plates on your device; camera images are not sent to our server. A plate you confirm may be added to a wash record.

How data is shared

Data is processed through the server and database infrastructure used to operate the app. If push notifications are enabled, the push token and notification content are sent to Expo's notification service for delivery. We do not share data with third parties for advertising. We may disclose data to authorities when legally required.

Security and retention

We do not store passwords in plain text. A password hash is stored on the server, and the mobile app uses protected device storage for the sign-in session. In production, the app connects to its server over HTTPS. Data is kept while the account is active and as needed to provide the service. Some records may be retained for a legally required period.

Your choices and deletion

You can manage push notifications in the app and change device permissions. You can request deletion of your account and its associated data through the account deletion page. We verify account ownership before processing a request. If any data must be retained by law, we will tell you about it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The latest version will appear on this page. Questions: yunote.app@gmail.com.
