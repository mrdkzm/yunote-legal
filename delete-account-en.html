Account deletion

To request deletion of your YuNote account and associated data, email yunote.app@gmail.com. Use “YuNote account deletion” as the subject and include the phone number used for your account. Do not send your password.

We will contact you to verify account ownership. Once the request is confirmed, we will process deletion of the account and its associated business, employee, vehicle, wash, payment, and notification records. If any data must be kept for legal reasons, we will explain what is retained and why.

Deleting your account also ends access to its records in the app. For more information, read our Privacy Policy.
